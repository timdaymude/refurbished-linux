This computer is ready.

Refurbished Linux 0.3.0 “First Desktop” — a small, quick system built for machines like this one.

1. Get online

Click the gear at the right of the bar, then Wi-Fi, and pick your network. The machine reconnects to it by itself from now on.

2. Open your things

Brave is the browser and it is already set up. Your Microsoft 365, Outlook, OneDrive and Teams shortcuts are in the launcher.

3. Add software when you need it

Software searches Debian’s catalogue only when you open it. Nothing is installed or updated behind your back.

4. Find anything

Press Super + Space to open the launcher, Alt + Tab to switch windows, and Super + L to lock the screen.

Microsoft 365 Outlook OneDrive Teams Cockpit
What is different here. There is no software constantly running in the background: no update checker, no indexer, no cloud sync, no telemetry. That is why this computer feels quick again. Windows programs do not run natively; Microsoft 365, Outlook, OneDrive and Teams all work in the browser.
